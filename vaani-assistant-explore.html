Vaani systems are designed for installation without complex rewiring. We blend Vaani’s proprietary AI engine with easily installed IoT devices to give you a smart home today, not next year.

Unlike bulky, expensive automation suites, Vaani Assistant focuses on modular, AI-powered control. Our devices learn your habits and automate tasks like lighting schedules, temperature adjustments, and security modes.

The core advantage is the deep integration with Kuchu.ai's context engine, allowing the assistant to anticipate needs based on external data (weather, calendar, time of day) and internal data (usage patterns).
